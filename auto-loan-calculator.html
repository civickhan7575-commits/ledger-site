Tips before you sign

  • Dealers often quote a monthly payment, not the price or rate — always ask for both separately
  • Shorter terms (3-4 years) mean higher payments but far less total interest
  • Get pre-approved by a bank or credit union first, so you can compare it against the dealer's offer
