How this calculator works

This tool uses the standard amortization formula lenders use to calculate a fixed-rate mortgage payment. It splits your loan amount (home price minus down payment) across your chosen term, applying your interest rate monthly.

Early in the loan, most of each payment goes toward interest. Later, more goes toward the principal balance — that shift is what the chart above shows.

What this doesn't include

  • Property taxes and homeowners insurance, which most lenders add to your monthly bill
  • Private mortgage insurance (PMI) if your down payment is under 20%
  • HOA fees, if applicable

Treat this as your baseline loan payment — your actual monthly bill from a lender will typically be somewhat higher once taxes and insurance are included.
