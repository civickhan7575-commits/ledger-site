Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Ledger.

Calculator inputs

Numbers you enter into any calculator (loan amount, interest rate, etc.) stay in your browser only. They are never sent to a server, stored, or shared. Closing or refreshing the page clears them.

Cookies and ads

This site may show ads served by third-party providers such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser or ad settings.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are useful, such as page views and general location by country. This data is aggregated and not tied to your identity.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
