About Ledger

Ledger is a set of free loan and mortgage calculators built for one reason: most people sign the biggest financial decisions of their life — a home, a car, a loan — without seeing the real math behind the payment they're told.

What we believe

A calculator should be honest, fast, and free. No email walls, no "create an account to see your results," no selling your data to lenders. You put in numbers, you see the truth, you leave.

How the calculators work

Every tool on this site uses the same standard amortization formula banks and lenders use internally. We show our assumptions clearly on each calculator page so you know exactly what's included — and what isn't.

What we're not

We're not a lender, broker, or financial advisor. Ledger doesn't collect applications, sell leads, or recommend specific products. The numbers here are estimates to help you walk into a real conversation with a lender already informed.
